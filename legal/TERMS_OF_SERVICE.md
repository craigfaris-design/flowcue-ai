## What FlowCue AI is

FlowCue AI is a real-time speech-following teleprompter: you paste in a
script, and as you speak, the app tracks your position and highlights where
you are — even if you pause, skip ahead, repeat yourself, or go off-script.
It uses live speech recognition (AssemblyAI, or your browser's built-in
recognizer as a fallback) to do this. See the
[Privacy Policy](./PRIVACY_POLICY.md) for exactly how your audio and data
are handled.

## Beta status

FlowCue AI is an early-stage product ("v0.2 (web MVP)" as shown in the app).
Features, accuracy, and availability may change without notice. Live cueing
accuracy depends on ambient noise, accent, internet connection, and the
third-party speech recognizers it uses — it is a rehearsal aid, not a
guaranteed-accurate transcription or a substitute for you knowing your own
material.
